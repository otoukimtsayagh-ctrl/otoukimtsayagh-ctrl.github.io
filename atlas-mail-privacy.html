Privacy Policy - Atlas Mail

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

At Atlas Mail, we respect your privacy. The application provides a free and secure temporary email service. We do not own private servers to store your messages; all operations are conducted directly with the email provider mail.tm via an open API encrypted with HTTPS.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data on our servers. The only information we store locally on your device includes: Temporary email address and password, Temporary Token access code, Theme settings and language preferences, and an optional local archive for 15 days.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your geographical location
  • We do not collect your contacts or photos
  • We do not sell your data or track you across other applications

4. Third-Party Services

a. mail.tm - Temporary Email Provider: Receives and sends messages on your behalf. Privacy Policy

b. Start.io - Advertising Network: We use their SDK to display ads. Start.io may collect advertising IDs, device types, and IP addresses anonymously. You can disable personalization from your Google settings. Start.io Policy

5. Ads and Consent

The application is free and supported by ads. Interstitial ads do not appear more than once every two minutes. The rewarded ad is optional.

6. Security & Retention Period

All communications are encrypted using HTTPS. Messages on mail.tm are automatically deleted after a few hours. The local archive is deleted after 15 days.

7. Your Rights & Children

You can delete your email, clear the archive, or uninstall the app to delete all local data. This application is not directed to children under 13 years of age.
