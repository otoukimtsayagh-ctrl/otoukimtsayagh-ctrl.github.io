ATLAS CV — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

This is the privacy notice for Atlas CV. The app does not require an account, and most resume creation and customization features run locally on your device.

1. What is stored locally?

The draft, saved CVs, photos, signature, customizations, favorites, application log and app settings are stored in the app’s private storage. The developer cannot view this content from inside the app.

2. Photos and exported files

A profile photo is copied into the app’s private space for use in the CV, and each saved CV keeps its own independent copy. PDF, Word, image and HTML files are created locally. They are not sent to another app or service unless you explicitly choose Share or Upload.

3. Career tools

Job Match, ATS Check, writing suggestions, Interview Prep, Cover Letter, Applications and Web CV operate on the device according to the current build. Job-ad text pasted into these tools is not sent to an external server by Atlas CV. The app does not send your CV content to an external AI service.

4. Ads and third parties

The app uses Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process an advertising ID, device and operating-system information, and technical or connectivity data needed to serve and measure ads, under Google’s Privacy Policy. Consent or privacy choices may appear depending on your region. Atlas CV does not send your CV text or photos to AdMob.

5. Network and external links

Internet access is used for ads, app/support pages, app-store services when available, and links you choose to open. When you tap WhatsApp, email or Share, the selected external app handles the next step under its own privacy policy.

6. Permissions

Depending on Android version and export method, the app may request access to selected photos/media or permission to save/share files. It does not use a permission for data unrelated to the feature you selected.

7. Backup and import

Export Data copies a JSON backup to the clipboard only when you ask. You are responsible for where you paste it and how you protect it. If you enable Android device backup, the operating system and Google may include app data under their backup controls; the developer does not manage or access that backup.

8. Deletion and retention

Settings → Reset Data removes the draft, saved CVs, customizations, local images and app-managed cache files. Uninstalling the app normally removes its private storage. Any file you exported or copied outside the app remains wherever you chose to place it.

9. Children

The app is a resume tool for job seekers and professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

10. Changes and contact

This notice may change when a new feature or service is added; the in-app date and the date on this page will be updated. Contact: otoukimt07@gmail.com.
