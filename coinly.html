عن التطبيق

Coinly تطبيق أندرويد لإدارة المال الشخصي والعائلي، طوّره راشد صياغ. صُمّم ليكون بسيطاً في الاستعمال ودقيقاً في الحساب، ويعمل بالعربية والإنجليزية والفرنسية.

ما يقدّمه

  • تسجيل المداخيل والمصاريف بفئات وحسابات متعددة، وتقسيم العملية الواحدة.
  • أقسام مستقلة لكل شخص تعيله، فتعرف كم أنفقت على الوالدين والأبناء والأسرة.
  • تفاصيل الشراء: المنتج والكمية وثمن الوحدة، مع تقرير يبيّن ما تنفقه على كل منتج.
  • ميزانيات بحدود وتنبيهات قبل التجاوز.
  • ديون في الاتجاهين مع تتبّع الدفعات، وأهداف ادّخار بمساهماتها.
  • الدفع المؤجل: تسجّل الشراء الآن ويُخصم من رصيدك يوم السداد.
  • تقارير بيانية وتصدير PDF و CSV.
  • استيراد من Excel أو CSV مع معالج يشرح كل خطوة قبل التنفيذ.
  • قفل بالبصمة أو برمز، وإخفاء المبالغ، ونسخ احتياطي محلي.

يعمل التطبيق دون إنترنت. Coinly أداة تتبّع مالي ولا يقدّم نصائح استثمارية أو ائتمانية.

About Coinly

Coinly is an Android app for personal and family money management, developed by Rachid Sayagh. It is built to be simple to use and precise in its arithmetic, and it works in Arabic, English and French.

What it offers

  • Income and expenses with multiple categories and accounts, and split entries.
  • A separate section for every person you support, so you know what goes to parents, children and family.
  • Purchase details — product, quantity, unit price — with a report showing what each product costs you.
  • Budgets with limits and alerts before you overspend.
  • Debts in both directions with payment tracking, and savings goals with contributions.
  • Deferred payment: record the purchase now, deduct it from your balance on the due date.
  • Visual reports and PDF / CSV export.
  • Import from Excel or CSV with a wizard that explains every step before it runs.
  • Fingerprint or PIN lock, hidden amounts, and local backups.

The app works offline. Coinly is a money tracker; it gives no investment or credit advice.

Privacy Policy

1. Your data stays on your device

Everything you record — income, expenses, categories, sections, accounts, budgets, debts, goals and purchase details — is stored locally in a database on your device only. None of it is sent to our servers, we hold no copy of it, and we cannot access it.

2. No account, no sign-in

The app asks for no email, no phone number and no account.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos or your behaviour inside the app. No analytics or crash-reporting tools send data to our servers.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads from the Google AdMob network to fund its development and keep it free: a banner at the bottom of the screen, occasional full-screen breaks, and optional rewarded ads used for some secondary features.

Google and its vendors receive your device advertising identifier, IP address and technical device information in order to select ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. This happens inside Google's library and we see none of it.

No financial data is ever sent to the ad network: not your amounts, categories, sections, debts, goals, or any text you entered.

In your Android settings you can delete the advertising identifier or turn off personalised ads, after which ads become generic rather than interest based. Use of advertising is also governed by Google's own privacy policy.

5. Permissions and why we ask

  • Internet — for ads and update checks. Recording entries does not need it.

  • Exact alarms — so reminders arrive on time. Optional.
  • Biometrics — to lock the app if you enable it. Handled by Android; never reaches the app.
  • Storage — to save backups and reports, and to read the import file you choose.

6. Backup, export and import

Files you create are saved on your device and are yours to handle. If you share one through another app, that app follows its own policy. An imported file is read on your device only and uploaded nowhere.

7. Works offline

The app works fully without a connection: recording, editing, deleting, budgets, debts, goals, reports and backups are all local. Only ads, update checks and rewarded ads need a connection, and losing it never stops you recording income or expenses.

8. No selling or sharing

We do not sell your financial data or share it with anyone, because we hold no copy of it.

9. Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

10. Your rights

Because the data lives on your device, it is entirely yours. You may delete any entry, erase all data from Settings, or uninstall the app — in every case the data is gone for good and no copy remains with us.
